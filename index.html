1 · What you might have been told

Every appointment you have had for five years, someone has been watching the clock.

Fifteen minutes, and the question is always the same. So what are we doing today.

You answered it, because you did not know what else to say. Nobody had told you what your hair was actually doing, so you picked something from the list and hoped.

Then you took the result home, lived with it, and quietly stopped mentioning it.

You did not imagine that.

2 · Here’s what’s actually happening

The water here has been working on your hair for years.

The supply here carries among the highest mineral levels in the world. It coats the hair with every single wash, like old make-up that never fully came off, blocks moisture from getting in, pulls the colour back out of it, and leaves a roughness no serum ever reaches, because the serum is working on the wrong surface.

3 · It’s no wonder you didn’t know

Nobody has been keeping that hour from you on purpose.

A senior stylist’s column is booked to the minute, because that is how a column pays for itself. When the day runs behind, and it always runs behind, the consultation is the first thing that gets cut, because it is the only part of the appointment with no product attached to it. Not your fault. Not the stylist’s. It is arithmetic.

That was never yours to work out alone.

So we built The Junior Experience around the columns that have room in them. The stylists working in it are junior, and we are not going to dress that up. But junior here is about demand, not ability: our senior columns are the most requested in the salon and they are priced that way, and hers is not there yet. She has trained in the same eight treatment categories, the same UAE diagnostics and the same consultation method, and she is signed off by a named senior on everything she offers you. Her book is still building, which is the only reason the hour exists at all.

This is not the same appointment for less money. It is a different appointment: less booked, not less able, and what her column can give you that a full one cannot, at any price, is the time. If the senior team is the right call for your hair, book them and we will tell you so.

Questions

The honest answers.

How long will I be there?
An hour, and it is a real hour: your stylist is not fitting you in around anybody else. If she finds something worth doing on the day and there is time, she will do it. If there is not, she will tell you that rather than start something she cannot finish properly. If you go on to book colour or smoothing afterwards, that is a separate appointment: a colour is around two and a half hours and smoothing is nearer three. The hour is not squeezed into the front of it.
How can you give me a full hour when nobody else does?
Because her column is quieter than the senior team’s, so in hers, nobody is watching the clock. A full column is booked to the minute, and the consultation is the first thing cut when the day runs behind. Hers has room in it, and the hour is ringfenced in the diary.
What actually happens in the hour?
Your stylist looks at your hair before anything begins. She touches it, asks what your water is like at home, and checks mineral build-up, bond damage, roughness and scalp. Then she tells you what she found in words that make sense, gives you a choice rather than a push, and writes the plan down. You take the card home, with your next dates in the diary.
Is a junior stylist less experienced?
Less booked, not less careful. Our senior team’s columns are the most requested in the salon and they’re priced that way: hers isn’t there yet. She’s signed off by a named senior on everything she’s offering you, before anything is mixed. And this isn’t about years: several of the team have been doing hair for well over a decade. What a full column genuinely cannot give you is the twenty minutes at the start, and that’s the thing you’d be booking.
What if I want the senior team instead?
Then book them. Our senior columns are the most requested in the salon, and they are priced that way. And if the senior team is the right call for your hair, we will tell you so in the chair.
What if it does not feel right afterwards?
You are covered by the Confidence Promise, in full, further up this page.
